Traditional Indonesian Eats 🍛
1. Nasi Campur (Mixed Rice): Traditional Balinese dish with rice, satay, sambal, veggies & egg. Try it at Warung Enak.
2. Seafood BBQ: Fresh caught Mahi-Mahi grilled on coconut husks at Jemeluk Bay. Served with spicy Sambal Matah.
3. Mie Goreng / Nasi Goreng: Classic fried noodles/rice. Found at almost every local Warung.
💰 ~HKD 30-50 / pax (Warungs)
💰 ~HKD 80-120 / pax (Seafood)
Free Time Ideas 🌴
When you have downtime, Amed is perfect for:
• Balinese Massage: Plentiful spas near La Paloma. (~HKD 70-100 per hour).
• Tirta Gangga Water Palace: Historic royal pools with koi fish. (30 mins scooter ride).
• Cafe Hopping: Hit up Rimba Cafe or Rimba for amazing coffee and brunch vibes.

Japanese Shipwreck Fun Dives
🌊 Site Intro: A sunken steel boat lying just 15m off the Banyuning shore. The top sits beautifully at just 2m deep, cascading down a sandy slope to 12m. Covered in gorgonians and sponges, it’s an absolute playground for shallow freediving and snorkeling!
Kick off the trip with relaxed freediving fun dives at the JP Shipwreck.

USAT Liberty Shipwreck (Scuba)
🌊 Site Intro: One of the world's most famous wrecks! A 120m American WWII cargo ship torpedoed in 1942. When Mt. Agung erupted in 1963, lava pushed the ship off the beach into the sea. It now rests on a black sand slope (4m to 30m) featuring massive swim-throughs and schools of bumphead parrotfish.
Swap out the long fins for a tank and explore Tulamben's deep history.
