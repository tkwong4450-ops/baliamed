Amed & Tulamben Quick Guide

Getting Around Amed

Scooter rental is ideal for quick runs along the coastal strip (Jl. I Ketut Natih). Drivers or private Jukung outrigger boats can be arranged directly from your beach warungs or hotel reception.

No-Fly & Off-Gassing Rule

Thursday, Nov 5 is strictly your no-diving window to safely off-gas nitrogen before flying home on Cathay CX784 on Nov 6 at 4:15 PM. Perfect day for maximum Padel & Volleyball!

Food & Drinks Vibe

Enjoy grilled mahi-mahi at beachfront warungs, fresh coconut water after court games, and cliffside sunset cocktails overlooking Mount Agung.
